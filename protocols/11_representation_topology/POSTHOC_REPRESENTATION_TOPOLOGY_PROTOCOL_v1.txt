POST-HOC REPRESENTATION AND TOPOLOGY ANALYSIS v1
=================================================

STATUS
------
EXPLORATORY / MECHANISTIC SECONDARY ANALYSIS.

This analysis is performed only after completion and freezing of the
original model-development pipeline.

NO model training, fine-tuning, model selection, architecture selection,
threshold tuning, human relabeling, or EventGold-guided optimization is
permitted in this analysis.

PURPOSE
-------
Characterize the mathematical structure of already-frozen neural
representations produced during development.

PRIMARY REPRESENTATION PAIR
---------------------------
XLMR_BASE embeddings
versus
XLMR_DAPT_INDUCTIVE embeddings

The two matrices represent the same 199 development articles in paired
order and each has 768 dimensions.

SECONDARY DESCRIPTIVE REPRESENTATIONS
-------------------------------------
Frozen Stage-B context-background embeddings.
Frozen verified-event prototype embeddings.

These secondary representations are described independently and are not
treated as paired observations with the 199-article representation set.

GEOMETRIC ANALYSES
------------------
1. Linear centered kernel alignment (linear CKA).
2. Spearman correlation of all pairwise representation distances.
3. k-nearest-neighbor retention for k = 5, 10, 20.
4. Effective rank from spectral entropy.
5. Participation ratio of the centered representation spectrum.
6. PCA eigenspectrum and fixed joint PCA coordinates for visualization.

Distance geometry uses row-wise L2-normalized vectors and Euclidean
distance in the normalized space. Therefore distance is monotonically
related to cosine similarity.

TOPOLOGICAL ANALYSES
--------------------
Vietoris-Rips persistent homology is computed from the precomputed
normalized-distance matrix using Ripser.

Homology dimensions:
H0 and H1.

For each representation and homology dimension:
- number of finite persistence intervals
- total persistence
- maximum persistence
- mean persistence
- persistent entropy

BASE vs DAPT persistence diagrams are additionally compared using
bottleneck distance.

TOPOLOGICAL STABILITY
---------------------
A paired subsampling analysis is performed.

Number of replicates: 1000
Subsample fraction: 0.80
Sampling: without replacement
Pairing: identical article indices in BASE and DAPT
RNG seed: 20261005

For every replicate, H0/H1 persistence summaries are recomputed.
DAPT-minus-BASE distributions and percentile 95% intervals are reported.

These intervals are exploratory stability summaries and are not treated
as preregistered confirmatory hypothesis tests.

EXISTING STATISTICAL DISTRIBUTIONS
----------------------------------
Previously frozen 10,000-replicate bootstrap/randomization distributions
from supervised representation probes and fold-safe axis ablations are
summarized without alteration.

INTERPRETATION CONSTRAINTS
--------------------------
1. LegacyAux-199 remains DEVELOPMENTAL evidence.
2. EventGold results are not used for model or representation selection.
3. This post-hoc analysis cannot convert developmental findings into
   independent validation.
4. Null topological/geometric contrasts are not evidence of equivalence.
5. Topological findings are exploratory and mechanistic.
6. No causal interpretation is permitted.
7. Seeds and folds are not treated as independent inferential subjects.
8. No additional human annotation is required or performed.
9. No model parameters are updated.
