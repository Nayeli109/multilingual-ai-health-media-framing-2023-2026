============================================

STATUS
------
PRE-SPECIFIED AND FROZEN BEFORE FINAL REFIT TRAINING AND BEFORE
ACCESS TO EVENTGOLD-35 CONTENT.

FINAL DEVELOPMENTAL ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------
STAGE_A_NO_EVENT

Developmental architecture selection is closed.

No V5, architecture search, hyperparameter search, threshold
optimization, or result-dependent model modification is
permitted before EventGold-35.

TRAINING DATA
-------------

Number of documents:
199

All 199 LegacyAux developmental documents are used for each
final refit.

No EventGold document is included in training, validation,
epoch selection, class weighting, tokenization, or any other
training operation.

0.10

- primary_frame
- stance
- misinformation_relation

MULTITASK OBJECTIVE
-------------------
All final models retain the frozen Stage-A multitask objective.

For every training document, class-weighted cross-entropy is
computed independently for all three tasks.

The three task losses are combined using their equal arithmetic
mean.

The target_task argument does NOT convert training into a
single-task procedure.

Its role in final refitting is solely to identify which
pre-frozen task-specific epoch schedule is being instantiated.

CLASS WEIGHTS
-------------
Class weights are recomputed from the complete 199-document
LegacyAux training set separately for each of the three tasks,
using the exact frozen Stage-A implementation.

LABEL MAPPING
-------------
Labels are converted to strings.

For each task, unique labels are sorted exactly as in frozen
Stage A and integer IDs are assigned in sorted order.

SCHEDULER
---------
The learning-rate scheduler horizon remains fixed at 8 epochs,
matching the frozen Stage-A refit implementation.

The model may stop before scheduler horizon 8 according to its
pre-frozen final_refit_epoch.

FINAL EPOCH RULE
----------------
Epochs are read exclusively from:

FINAL_REFIT_EPOCHS_v1.csv

That table was frozen before final training and before EventGold
access.

For every task x seed configuration, final_refit_epoch is the
ROUND_HALF_UP median of the four Stage-A selected epochs.

No validation or early stopping is performed during final
refitting.

FINAL CONFIGURATIONS
--------------------
3 target tasks x 5 seeds = 15 logical final models.

Each logical model is trained independently from the same
XLM-R base initialization procedure.

Because the underlying objective is multitask, two logical
task/seed configurations having the same seed and the same final
epoch are expected to be numerically identical under strict
determinism. They remain separate logical configurations because
the downstream frozen ensemble is task-specific.

CHECKPOINTING
-------------
Each successful final refit stores:

- model_state_dict_v1.pt
- label_maps_v1.json
- document_chunking_v1.csv
- run_summary_final_refit_v1.json

The checkpoint contains the complete trained state_dict and
configuration metadata required for later inference.

No optimizer or scheduler state is needed after successful
training.

FINAL MODEL SELECTION
---------------------
There is no model selection after final refitting.

All five seed-specific checkpoints belonging to each task are
retained.

No final-refit training metric will be used to select, exclude,
weight, or modify a seed.

EVENTGOLD
---------
EventGold-35 remains:

SEALED_NOT_ACCESSED

throughout protocol creation, trainer creation, preflight,
final refit training, checkpoint audit, and checkpoint freeze.

EventGold may be opened only after all 15 final checkpoints have
successfully completed and the final checkpoint package has been
audited and frozen.
