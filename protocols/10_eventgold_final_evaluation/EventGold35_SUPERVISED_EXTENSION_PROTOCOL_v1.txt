EVE-FRAME — EVENTGOLD35 PROSPECTIVE SUPERVISED EXTENSION PROTOCOL v1
===================================================================

STATUS
------
FROZEN BEFORE CREATION OF ANY NEW EVENTGOLD SUPERVISED LABEL.

This protocol defines a separately versioned prospective supervised
extension of the original EventGold35 benchmark.

It does NOT retroactively redefine the original EventGold35 annotation
schema.

The original attempted supervised EventGold evaluation was formally
closed because the original CSV did not contain the three Stage-A
supervised targets.

At the time this protocol is frozen:

- no EventGold model prediction exists;
- no EventGold performance metric exists;
- no new EventGold Stage-A label exists;
- the final Stage-A architecture remains unchanged;
- the 15 final Stage-A checkpoints remain frozen.


1. SOURCE DOCUMENT SET
----------------------
Source benchmark:

01_event_aware_v3/00_frozen_benchmark/EventGold35_FINAL.csv

Expected documents:
35

The original 35 doc_id values define the supervised extension.

No document may be added, removed, replaced, resampled, or selected
according to model behavior.

The original EventGold semantic annotation fields must NOT be used to
derive the new Stage-A target labels.


2. PURPOSE
----------
The extension provides independent human labels for the three already
frozen Stage-A tasks:

1. primary_frame
2. stance
3. misinformation_relation

The taxonomies are inherited from the historical human annotation
codebook and from the final Stage-A training label spaces.

No taxonomy may be changed after annotation begins.


3. MODEL-TEXT CONSTRUCTION
--------------------------
The original EventGold file does not contain the historical dapt_text
field.

Therefore a prospective deterministic compatibility rule is defined
before supervised annotation.

For each document:

Step 1:
Normalize article_excerpt_3500 by replacing line breaks with spaces,
stripping leading/trailing whitespace, and collapsing repeated
whitespace.

Step 2:
If normalized article_excerpt_3500 is non-empty, use it as dapt_text.

Step 3:
Otherwise apply the same normalization to article_excerpt_1800.
If non-empty, use article_excerpt_1800 as dapt_text.

Step 4:
Otherwise apply the same normalization to title and use title as
dapt_text.

No other EventGold field may contribute to dapt_text.

In particular, the following fields are prohibited as model-text
inputs:

problem_definition
problem_definition_span
causal_actor
causal_actor_type
consequence_type
consequence_description
evaluation_tone
remedy_present
remedy_description
remedy_actor
responsibility_actor
responsibility_actor_type
epistemic_certainty
epistemic_cues
evidence_source_type
evidence_source_actor
evidence_span
health_claim_present
health_claim_text
ai_system_or_company
coder_confidence
coder_notes


4. HUMAN ANNOTATION TEXT
------------------------
The historical human-validation pipeline annotated each document using
its title and a text excerpt.

The historical choose_excerpt() implementation normalized whitespace
and truncated the selected article text to the first 1400 characters.

To preserve that annotation precedent:

Human annotators will see:

- title;
- the first 1400 characters of normalized dapt_text.

The displayed human excerpt is therefore:

annotation_excerpt = dapt_text[:1400]

The original EventGold semantic annotations must remain hidden from the
annotators.


5. UNIT OF ANALYSIS
-------------------
One news/media article.


6. TASK 1 — PRIMARY FRAME
--------------------------
Exactly one label must be selected.

Allowed labels:

1. clinical innovation and diagnostic benefit

Use when the article frames AI as improving diagnosis, medical imaging,
clinical workflows, screening, treatment support, or healthcare
efficiency.

2. health safety risk and medical harm

Use when the article emphasizes risks to patients, incorrect medical
advice, unsafe deployment, bias, hallucinations, harm, or clinical
unreliability.

3. misinformation, deepfakes, and fraud

Use when the article focuses on false information, synthetic media,
scams, fake ads, deepfakes, manipulated content, or misleading health
claims.

4. regulation, governance, and policy

Use when the article focuses on laws, AI Act, government regulation,
institutional governance, standards, enforcement, or policy debates.

5. public trust and accountability

Use when the article focuses on trust, transparency, responsibility,
accountability, institutional credibility, or public confidence.

6. access, inequality, and digital divide

Use when the article focuses on unequal access, Global South/Global
North gaps, rural/urban disparities, cost barriers, language barriers,
or technological dependence.

7. labor automation and professional displacement

Use when the article focuses on job replacement, professional roles,
productivity, workers, lawyers, clinicians, students, or automation of
labor.

8. mental health and therapy risk

Use when the article focuses on AI therapy, chatbots for mental health,
suicide, psychosis, emotional dependence, psychological risk, or
psychiatric concerns.

9. other / unclear

Use only when none of the above labels clearly applies.


7. TASK 2 — STANCE TOWARD AI IN HEALTH
---------------------------------------
Exactly one label must be selected.

Allowed labels:

1. optimistic about AI in health

The article mainly emphasizes benefits, promise, innovation, progress,
or positive transformation.

2. cautious or mixed about AI in health

The article presents both benefits and risks, or uses a balanced or
uncertain tone.

3. critical of AI risks in health

The article mainly emphasizes danger, harm, misinformation, fraud,
bias, or negative consequences.

4. focused on regulation and governance

The article is mainly about rules, regulation, policy, governance, or
institutional oversight rather than clearly positive or negative
evaluation.

5. unclear / not applicable

Use when the stance cannot be inferred.


8. TASK 3 — RELATION TO MISINFORMATION
---------------------------------------
Exactly one label must be selected.

Allowed labels:

1. direct misinformation focus

The article is mainly about misinformation, deepfakes, fake ads,
scams, false health claims, or propaganda.

2. indirect misinformation concern

Misinformation is mentioned as a risk or background concern, but is not
the main focus.

3. no clear misinformation relation

The article does not meaningfully discuss misinformation.

4. unclear / not applicable

Use only when the available title/excerpt does not support a reliable
classification.


9. EXCLUDED HUMAN TASKS
-----------------------
Secondary frame is not part of the external evaluation.

Main actor is not part of the external evaluation.

Neither will be annotated for the supervised extension.


10. BLIND INDEPENDENT ANNOTATION
--------------------------------
Two human annotators are required:

Annotator A
Annotator B

Each annotator must independently annotate all 35 documents.

Before both independent annotation passes are frozen, neither annotator
may see:

- the other annotator's labels;
- Stage-A predictions;
- probabilities or logits;
- zero-shot predictions;
- historical machine labels;
- final Stage-A development results;
- EventGold model predictions;
- EventGold performance;
- original EventGold semantic annotation fields.

Annotation order must be deterministic and frozen before annotation.

The two annotators may use only:

- document title;
- frozen 1400-character annotation excerpt;
- this frozen codebook.


11. ANNOTATION NOTES
--------------------
Each annotator may optionally record a free-text note describing:

- ambiguity;
- insufficient context;
- mixed framing;
- translation uncertainty;
- reason for choosing an unclear category.

Notes must never contain model predictions.


12. FREEZE OF INDEPENDENT ANNOTATIONS
-------------------------------------
Annotator A's complete file must be frozen and SHA256-hashed.

Annotator B's complete file must be frozen and SHA256-hashed.

No agreement statistic may be used to alter an annotator's completed
independent labels.

Both independent files must be frozen before adjudication begins.


13. INTER-ANNOTATOR AGREEMENT
-----------------------------
Agreement is calculated before adjudication.

For each of the three tasks report:

- exact raw agreement;
- Cohen's kappa;
- number of agreements;
- number of disagreements.

Agreement is descriptive and does not determine whether a task is
retained or removed.


14. ADJUDICATION
----------------
Only task-level disagreements are adjudicated.

For a task where Annotator A and Annotator B agree:

final label = agreed label.

For a task where Annotator A and Annotator B disagree:

the discrepant labels and the same frozen title/excerpt are reviewed
during adjudication and one final label from the frozen taxonomy is
selected.

No model prediction, probability, zero-shot output, or original
EventGold semantic annotation may be shown during adjudication.


15. FINAL SUPERVISED LABELS
---------------------------
The final output columns will be:

final_primary_frame_validated
final_stance_validated
final_misinformation_relation_validated

For agreement cases:
the shared independent human label is used.

For disagreement cases:
the adjudicated label is used.

All 35 documents must contain exactly one valid label for each of the
three tasks before the supervised extension is frozen.


16. SUPERVISED EXTENSION FREEZE
-------------------------------
Before model inference, the following must be frozen and SHA256-hashed:

- protocol;
- deterministic text-construction implementation;
- blind annotation packet;
- Annotator A completed labels;
- Annotator B completed labels;
- agreement report;
- adjudication file;
- final 35-document supervised extension;
- label-space manifest.

After this freeze, target labels cannot be modified.


17. FINAL MODEL
--------------
Selected architecture:

STAGE_A_NO_EVENT

No retraining is permitted.

No hyperparameter change is permitted.

No architecture change is permitted.

Exactly the already frozen 15 final checkpoints will be used:

3 tasks × 5 seeds.


18. FINAL INFERENCE
-------------------
Each task is evaluated using its five already frozen task-specific
checkpoints.

For each document and checkpoint:

- tokenize frozen dapt_text using the frozen Stage-A tokenizer and
  preprocessing;
- compute class probabilities;
- retain the complete probability vector.

For each task:

- take the elementwise arithmetic mean of the five seed probability
  vectors;
- select argmax of the mean probability vector.

No threshold tuning is permitted.

No calibration is permitted.

No checkpoint removal is permitted.

No seed reweighting is permitted.

No majority voting is permitted.


19. PRIMARY EXTERNAL METRIC
---------------------------
Primary metric for each task:

Macro-F1.

The three tasks are reported separately.

No pooled three-task success criterion is defined.


20. SECONDARY METRICS
---------------------
For each task report:

- balanced accuracy;
- accuracy;
- weighted-F1.


21. UNCERTAINTY
---------------
For Macro-F1 only:

95% percentile bootstrap confidence interval.

Resampling unit:
article.

Resampling:
stratified within the observed final gold classes of that task.

Replicates:
10,000.

The original frozen EventGold bootstrap RNG seeds are retained:

primary_frame:
2026092701

stance:
2026092702

misinformation_relation:
2026092703

Quantiles:

2.5%
97.5%

The intervals are descriptive.

No p-values are computed.

No randomization tests are computed.

No BH correction is applied.


22. PROHIBITED POST-ANNOTATION CHANGES
--------------------------------------
After annotation begins, the following are prohibited:

- changing a taxonomy;
- merging classes;
- dropping classes because they are rare;
- relabeling classes based on model behavior;
- modifying dapt_text construction;
- changing annotation excerpt length;
- changing checkpoints;
- changing seeds;
- retraining;
- changing metrics;
- selecting documents based on predictions;
- inspecting performance and then changing the evaluation procedure.


23. INTERPRETATION
------------------
This benchmark must be described as:

"a prospectively human-annotated supervised extension of the previously
held-out EventGold35 document set."

It must NOT be described as though the three Stage-A target labels were
part of the original EventGold35 annotation schema.

The original failed evaluation remains permanently documented as a
schema/target incompatibility event with no performance result.
