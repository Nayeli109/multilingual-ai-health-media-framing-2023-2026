=====================================================
SCIENTIFIC CONCLUSION
=====================================================

Full supervised fine-tuning did not provide evidence that inductive
domain-adaptive pretraining yields a general downstream advantage over
the original multilingual initialization.

Effects were task-dependent:
- negative numerical shift for primary_frame;
- modest positive numerical shift for stance;
- essentially unchanged ensemble performance for
  misinformation_relation.

None of the three predeclared task-level Macro-F1 contrasts was
supported after paired uncertainty estimation and BH-FDR correction.

The dominant downstream limitation was not overall accuracy alone.
Multiple minority classes remained completely unrecovered under both
initializations, producing substantial divergence between overall
accuracy and Macro-F1.

Accordingly, no additional post-hoc attempt will be made to optimize
or rescue DAPT on LegacyAux-199.

The BASE-vs-DAPT block is retained as a frozen benchmark for subsequent
architectural experiments.
