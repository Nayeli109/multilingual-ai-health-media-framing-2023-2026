=====================================================
DEVELOPMENT INTERPRETATION
=====================================================

Stage A demonstrates that shared multi-task representation learning
can materially alter minority-class discrimination relative to
independent single-task fine-tuning.

The effect is task-dependent.

Primary-frame classification shows the strongest and confirmatorily
supported development-set improvement.

For stance and misinformation relation, gains in Macro-F1 / balanced
accuracy are accompanied by redistribution away from dominant classes,
and the task-level Macro-F1 contrasts are not supported by the
predeclared randomization/FDR procedure.

No claim of independent validation is made.

Stage A was designed after inspection of earlier LegacyAux error
structure and is therefore development evidence.

=====================================================
NEXT ARCHITECTURAL RULE
=====================================================

Stage A is now frozen as the NO_EVENT comparator.

Subsequent EVE-FRAME development must preserve the Stage-A core,
training regime, target-task folds, optimizer settings, class weighting,
and evaluation protocol.

The next experimental component should change only the event-context
mechanism wherever technically possible, so its incremental contribution
can be isolated against Stage A.

No reactive modification of Stage-A loss weighting or task heads will
be made based on these results.
